# Email Setup Guide

## Issue
The user says their email is not syncing, cannot send messages, or cannot receive messages on their phone or computer.

## Basic Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the user’s email address is entered correctly.
2. Check the internet connection on the device.
3. Ask whether the problem affects sending, receiving, or both.
4. Remove and re-add the email account if the sync issue continues.
5. Restart the email application or the device.
6. If multi-factor authentication is enabled, confirm that the user completed the login process correctly.
7. If the issue still continues, escalate the case to the messaging or IT support team.

## Recommended Helpdesk Response
If a user reports that email is not syncing, first verify the account settings and internet connection. If needed, remove and re-add the email account. If the issue continues after that, escalate the case for further support.

## Escalation Conditions
Escalate the case if:
- the user cannot send or receive messages after re-adding the account
- the issue affects multiple users
- the email server appears unavailable
